Together Task Hub privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026. Publisher: Together_Today. Applies to the reviewed Together Task Hub release candidate.

Together stores tasks, notes, saved file metadata, conversation history, settings and backup information in your computer's user storage. Saved files and backups are kept locally unless you choose a shared/synchronized folder or send files to a paired computer. Your folder provider, such as OneDrive, processes synchronized content under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you enable AI and send a question, Together sends the question, recent conversation context, basic computer status (including the computer name and counts of connected computers, files and backups), and intermediate helper replies to OpenAI. The app's advice workflow makes multiple AI requests for an answer. Account access and processing are governed by your chosen OpenAI service and account settings. Using AI does not automatically attach all your saved files. Do not submit sensitive information you do not intend to share with that service.

Sign in with ChatGPT uses the bundled official sign-in runtime. Its private account state is stored separately from app files and shared content. An optional API key is used for requests to OpenAI; API billing is separate from ChatGPT plan access.

Phone access uses a short-lived pairing code and a remembered browser cookie when requested. Together stores a hash of the remembered token on the computer, rather than the token itself. You can revoke remembered phones in desktop settings. Remote networking providers, when configured, process connection traffic according to their policies. Keep the phone address and browser session private.

The optional Companion adapter sends selected status, app-opening and media requests to the configured agent on the same computer. It reads private local connection credentials to authenticate. Those credentials are not sent to AI as part of the basic status context. Together does not automatically execute AI recommendations.

This reviewed build has no publisher-operated analytics or advertising endpoint identified in its source. Local logs and service-provider logs may contain operational information. This statement does not override the policies of Microsoft Store, OpenAI, your synchronized-folder provider or your remote-access provider.

You control the files, shared folders and accounts you connect. Disconnect AI and revoke phones through desktop settings. Delete local app data and backups to remove locally stored content after retaining any copies you need; remove synchronized copies through the relevant provider. Deleting local copies does not automatically erase copies on paired computers or provider systems. No fixed retention schedule or remote deletion service is implemented by Together.
