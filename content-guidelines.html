Together content guidelines

Together is a productivity workspace for your tasks, files and notes, with optional AI advice. Share only with computers and phones you trust and have paired. Together has no public feed.

Do not use Together to share illegal content, child sexual exploitation material, threats, harassment, hate targeting protected groups, non-consensual intimate content, malware, or content you lack permission to share. Do not use AI to create harmful or prohibited content.

AI replies may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Check advice before acting on it. Together does not automatically perform AI recommendations. Provider safeguards do not guarantee that every reply is suitable.

Report a concern

Use “Report this reply” beside an assistant answer, or “Report shared content” on My files & notes. Describe the concern and include only the information you choose. Reports open an email draft to justin_wright88@outlook.com; you must send the email to deliver it. If no email app is configured, copy your report into your usual email service.

The publisher reviews received reports and may request more information or address a concern through changes to the app or guidance to the affected user. This is not automatic moderation, an emergency service, or a guarantee of response time. The publisher cannot remotely remove private files on your computer. You control locally saved files and shared-folder copies; revoke untrusted pairing or pause sharing in desktop Settings and remove unwanted files through your computer or storage provider.

Microsoft may require content or features to be removed or disabled. Publisher review and corrective action must follow applicable requirements. No app-specific automatic content filter or public-user blocking service is provided.
